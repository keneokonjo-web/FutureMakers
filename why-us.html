Why choose us

Learning that feels like making.

FutureMakers Academy runs short, live, online workshops for children aged 6–14. Children come away having made something of their own, and parents always know what is happening and what they are paying for.

See the workshops Play Maker Match

Six reasons families choose us.

  • Hands-on, not lecture-style

    Each workshop is a live, guided session of about 75 minutes. Children try short activities, build an idea step by step and finish with something they can share with their family.

  • Two very different workshops

    Saturday’s Young Entrepreneurs Lab is about business ideas and a short pitch. Sunday’s AI Creators Lab is about creating with AI. Book one day, or both.

  • No experience needed

    Both workshops start from the beginning and assume no experience of business or AI. Activities are adapted for Juniors (6–9) and Seniors (10–14).

  • Parents stay in charge

    Registration is completed by a parent or guardian. We do not ask for children’s names or dates of birth, and we only collect what we need to register you.

  • Clear, honest pricing

    Prices are shown in naira, pounds or dollars with no exchange-rate surprises. The full weekend costs less than booking both days separately, and nothing is ever charged automatically.

  • Every payment checked by a person

    You pay by bank transfer or Revolut, send us your receipt, and a real person verifies it before your place is confirmed. Your registration reference keeps everything easy to track.
