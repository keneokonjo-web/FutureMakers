Young Entrepreneurs Lab

What if your idea became a real business? Discover how businesses work, invent something of your own, decide who it is for and learn how a great idea can become something people want.

What children will do

  1. Discover how businesses make money.
  2. Find a problem worth solving.
  3. Invent a product or service.
  4. Understand customers and pricing.
  5. Create a simple brand.
  6. Present a mini business idea.
